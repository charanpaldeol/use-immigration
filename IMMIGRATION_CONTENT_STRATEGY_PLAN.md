## PART 1: MARKET RESEARCH FINDINGS

### A. CANADA-WIDE IMMIGRATION TRENDS (2026)

#### 1. Permanent Economic Immigration - HIGHEST PRIORITY
- **64%** of all immigration will be economic category (2027-2028)
- Focus on high-skilled workers and talent retention
- Emphasis on workers in **in-demand sectors**

#### 2. Top In-Demand Sectors
| Sector | Key Roles | Relevance |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Healthcare** | Nurse practitioners, dentists, pharmacists, chiropractors | Growing demand due to aging population |
| **Technology/IT** | Software engineers, cybersecurity specialists, AI researchers | Digital transformation, AI investment |
| **Trades** | Carpenters, plumbers, machinists, electricians | Infrastructure expansion projects |
| **Clean Energy** | Engineers, technicians | Government green energy initiatives |
| **Construction** | Project managers, skilled trades | Housing and infrastructure needs |

#### 3. Key Policy Changes Effective 2026-2027
- **TR-to-PR Fast Track**: 33,000 temporary workers → permanent residence (2026-2027)
- **Rural Area Focus**: Workers in non-major-city areas prioritized
- **Temporary Resident Reductions**: Fewer temporary admissions, more permanent pathways
- **Family Sponsorship**: 84,000 family class admissions planned; Super Visa enhanced
- **Express Entry**: Highest proportion dedicated to skilled workers

### B. NIAGARA-SPECIFIC OPPORTUNITIES (2026)

#### Regional Advantages
1. **Excluded from TR-to-PR Restrictions**: Major cities (Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary) excluded; Niagara IS eligible
2. **31% Increase in OINP Allocations**: Ontario received +4,369 additional nominations (10,750 → 14,119)
3. **Labor Shortages**: Regional demand for skilled workers exceeds major urban centers
4. **Newer Market**: Less competitive than Calgary/Toronto for qualified workers
5. **Growing Infrastructure**: Niagara Falls tourism, healthcare, tech sectors expanding

#### Target Industries in Niagara Region
- Healthcare (nursing shortages, seniors care)
- Hospitality & Tourism (post-pandemic recovery)
- Manufacturing & Engineering
- Agriculture & Food Production
- Professional Services
